SESAA Soccer

Fast reminders for league games and Celebration Play Days. Confirm last-minute changes with the convenor or SESAA.

Before a Celebration Play Day

  • One tournament per player: SESAA says players may participate in only one soccer tournament.
  • Recommended roster: 15 players per team; 11 play at a time.
  • Bring one size 4 game ball.
  • Goalkeeper: different-coloured top from teammates.
  • Coach meeting: be there 10 minutes before the first game.
  • Referee: plan to bring an experienced parent or secondary-school student unless the convenor confirms otherwise.

SESAA's page contains both statements: convenors are responsible for Celebration Play Day referees, and each team is asked to bring an experienced referee. Treat that as a reason to confirm rather than assume.

Game reminders

  • Playday game: two 10-minute halves, subject to the number of teams.
  • Unlimited substitutions, but only during a stoppage in play.
  • Opponents must be 10 yards from the ball at kickoff.
  • Extreme lateness can result in a forfeit.
  • For league play, the home team should supply the referee.

Our current logistics

  • Playdays: October 15 · Newton Athletic Park and October 16 · Cloverdale Athletic Park.
  • Keep recruiting approved drivers for the playdays even though regular-game transportation is covered.
  • Before leaving: roster/permissions, jerseys, ball, goalkeeper top, referee plan, driver plan, water and first-aid supplies.
