Comparing numbers · 8:45–9:35

Briefly review rounding, then introduce comparing whole numbers and decimals. Line up place values and compare from left to right. In 4,582 and 4,528, the thousands and hundreds match; 8 tens is greater than 2 tens, so 4,582 > 4,528. For 4.70 and 4.65, compare tenths: 7 > 6, so 4.70 > 4.65.

Try 4,209 ___ 4,290 and 6.4 ___ 6.04. Explain which place helped you decide. Choose page 1 from either booklet or the English–Korean packet below.

Nevermoor · 9:35–10:20

Read from the class’s confirmed bookmark. Choose a character’s thought, feeling or goal and support your idea with a detail from the part read today. Write, draw or tell a short response and one question. The English–Korean packet has a response page on page 5.

Fair communities · 10:50–11:50

What makes a community fair, and what could improve? Consider a shared play area used by the same group every recess. What change could help people take part, who could help decide, and what do we still need to ask? Different solutions may have good reasons.

Choose a public issue. Write or dictate a paragraph naming who is affected, one possible improvement with a reason, and one inquiry question. Share with a partner and revise a sentence when feedback helps.
