Orange Shirt Day art examples

Finish both your Discovery and Truth and Reconciliation booklet work first. Your agreed supported work counts. Join shared lessons when they begin.

Return to Phyllis Webstad’s story and what we have learned about residential schools. Choose a design that helps you explain why every child matters, why listening matters, or why children’s identities and belonging must be respected.

Make your meaning clear

  1. Choose a simple symbol: a heart, book, speech bubbles, a circle of hearts, a home or a seedling.
  2. Sketch lightly on your paper shirt, then add clear outlines and a short message.
  3. Explain: “I chose ___ because ___. It connects to our reading when ___.” Point, speak, dictate or write; a partner can help record your explanation.

Use your own design. Learn about specific Indigenous artworks from their artists rather than copying cultural symbols.
