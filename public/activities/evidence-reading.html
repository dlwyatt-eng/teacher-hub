Our question

What does this account help us understand about why remembrance matters?

  1. Read or listen to the passage your teacher has selected. Name the author or speaker.
  2. Discuss your idea with a partner. Choose a specific detail that supports it.
  3. Explain how that detail helps us understand. Point, speak, draw, dictate or write.

I learned… The text or speaker tells us… This helps us understand… because…

A model using Phyllis Webstad’s account

Idea: Listening to Survivors helps us understand the harm caused by residential schools.

Detail: Phyllis describes her orange shirt being taken and feeling that her feelings did not matter.

Explanation: Her account shows how the treatment of a child affected her sense of worth. Remembering includes listening to these experiences and respecting children’s identities and feelings.

This model paraphrases Phyllis’s own account, published by the Orange Shirt Society. Read the selected passage together. Use a detail from your own assigned reading in your response.

Check your response

  • I understood the account and named the source.
  • I chose a relevant detail.
  • I explained what the detail helps us understand.
  • My audience can follow my thinking.
