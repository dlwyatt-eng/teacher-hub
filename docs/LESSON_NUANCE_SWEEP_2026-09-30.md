Shared teacher discussion prompts now distinguish disciplinary thinking across all eight subjects. The optional SchoolAI discussion handoff no longer demands a single agreed answer when supported interpretations differ.

Main distinctions:

- Clear task instructions remain; a model demonstrates a move without fixing the only possible interpretation.
- Readers support a reading, acknowledge a complicating detail, and distinguish purposeful ambiguity from confusion.
- Poetry can use personification and figurative language. Students explain its relation to literal scientific claims.
- Arts evidence centres on technique, intention, audience response, and choices. Full printable organizers remain supplied; filling and decorating every box is not the assessment target.
- Mathematical answers remain exact. Strategy selection, justification, counterexamples, graph purposes, and probability variation provide depth. Pack-and-sync student finish checks no longer reveal the solution before the task; existing teaching models and answer routes remain.
- Access, participation, design, and career tasks consider whose experience was tested, less visible contributions, and what an observation cannot establish.
- Science models and Social Studies responses are judged for a stated question and context, including conditions under which another choice could be more useful.
