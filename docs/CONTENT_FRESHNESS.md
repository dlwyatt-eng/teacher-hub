# Public hub freshness check

The Teacher Hub is the canonical source for the public learning window. On each meaningful class-plan or family update, change the dated text in `content/current-learning-window-v2.json`, run `npm run sync:public-window`, and copy the generated manifest to Learn Hub. Do not present a planned activity as completed teaching.

Before publishing the three hubs, check:

1. The student Shape of the Day titles, times and links are still accurate; keep archived days intact.
2. The Learn Hub class-update date and daily plan are current, or the old-update notice is visible. Confirm school dates with the school source; do not invent family actions or teacher reply-time promises.
3. The Learn and Equity footers' dated review lines reflect a real review of each site; update them when content changes.
4. The October 17 Surrey and October 24 B.C. elections remain distinct. Confirm dates and candidate/result claims against the City of Surrey and Elections BC. After each election, verify the homepage switches to accountability/evergreen content, and do not call mock votes confirmed without school confirmation.
5. Open a fresh direct link into the public Truth and Reconciliation route, one representative printable PDF, and the family mailto link. Check the social-preview image URLs and cross-hub links.
