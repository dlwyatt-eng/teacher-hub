# Math worksheets and supplied resources — 13 September 2026

The teacher found that worksheets, games and teaching files were still buried and that the Hub's math lessons did not add enough to the existing paper resources. This repair brings materials to the top of the lesson and gives the Hub a more specific supplementary role.

## Changes

- Direct publisher worksheet, video, lesson/exercise and teacher-key links appear before the long math plan. The full investigation plan remains available in a disclosure.
- Games/tools have visible launch controls, actual classroom directions and a paper alternative. The sale lesson selects percent/budget activities rather than unrelated decimal practice.
- Eighteen original discussion companions address a misconception, comparison, explanation or transfer. Each contains a concrete prompt, teacher selection step for practice, an independent check and controlled answer reveal. Scoreboard Rules and Sale Lab have their own focus rather than inheriting a broad unit's first pack.
- Projector mode has a compact resource bar and displays the active math task before optional help. Teacher Math Antics answer-key links and saved-file links are omitted from that resource bar. These display choices do not implement authentication.
- Saved Resources is a main navigation entry: 59 distinct uploaded files, searchable notes, subject filters, source links, fit/adaptation guidance and explicit limited-review labels. The privately stored archive and review guide have direct links there.
- Publisher PDFs were downloaded through the signed-in account, verified and stored separately in the user's Drive. No purchased PDFs or answer keys were copied into this public repository. Existing source-file permissions are unchanged.
