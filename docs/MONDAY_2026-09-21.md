## 9:20–10:20 am · Math · whole-number place value

Full one-hour introduction; do not rush into decimals. Suggested pacing: 10-minute opening/model, 10-minute optional Math Antics explanation with pauses, 15-minute guided work, 20-minute practice and extension, 5-minute check. Use the existing linked Magnitude Gallery resources selectively for whole-number place value, not its entire sequence. Math Antics source: https://mathantics.com/lesson/place-value. Complete no-video route: draw columns thousands, hundreds, tens, ones; place 4,582 and expand it to 4,000 + 500 + 80 + 2. Ask what the 5 represents: 500. Explain that each place to the left is ten times the value of the place to its right; regroup ten ones into one ten. Partners represent 6,307 = 6,000 + 300 + 7 and explain zero tens. Practise reading, making, comparing and expanding whole numbers; offer fewer places and manipulatives or larger whole numbers as appropriate. Whole-number extension: use 2, 4, 6 and 8 once each to make the greatest (8,642) and smallest (2,468) four-digit numbers and explain. Exit check: value of 6 in 6,042 is 6,000; value of 6 in 2,615 is 600. Keep responses to choose the next lesson. Larger-number extensions are optional; decimal place value waits until later in the week.
