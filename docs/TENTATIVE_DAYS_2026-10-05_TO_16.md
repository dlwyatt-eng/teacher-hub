## Before using a draft

1. Check actual Mathematics evidence, novel bookmark and inquiry stopping point
2. Keep the student-selected Action Pack available through the teacher’s existing access; preview its chosen activity
3. Select a paper or projector route and have ordinary paper/pencils ready; student devices, new posts and account setup are not needed
4. Confirm school changes and any LST block; move unfinished work rather than compress it
5. Review the chosen activity on the actual projector and print route before classroom use

No full book text, licensed Action Pack content, student records or teacher login links are copied into the plans. Public reflections should remain general; individual assessment records stay private.
